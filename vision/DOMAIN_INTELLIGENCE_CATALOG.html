The living loop

A catalog that gets smarter with use.

Packs aren't published once and frozen. Every consult that misses, every SME who disagrees, every correction becomes a governed change that ships as the next version — with the audit trail intact.

1
Consult

An agent queries a pack. CTX flags when the served knowledge didn't actually answer — a usage gap.

2
Mission

The gap becomes a Forge mission. An SME spars with the machine and supplies the missing judgment.

3
Govern

The contribution is a Delta: proposed → reviewed → approved. Nothing enters a pack ungoverned.

4
Evolve

Approved Deltas promote to ACTIVE and recompile. v0.1.0 → v0.2.0, re-sealed.

5
Serve

The next consult is grounded in the new knowledge. The catalog shows both versions, diffable.

Proven, two ways. A cyberattack-disruption query that v0.1.0 answered generically produced a Forge mission → evolve_pack → commercial_analytics@0.2.0, whose answer is now grounded in a new rule_cyber_disruption heuristic. Offline regression + a live Haiku-4.5 driver both confirm it.

Drop-a-file expansion

A new function is a single YAML dropped into ontology/commercial/. The forecasting module — LOE erosion curves, demand sensing, analog launch trajectories, scenario sensitivity — joined the pack with zero code change. Functions version together; the catalog just shows a new slice.

Governance is the moat

Every ACTIVE artifact carries a real delta_id in its history — even bootstrap seeds. A transition to ACTIVE/VERIFIED without a governing Delta raises. "Governed" is an invariant here, not a convention.

The platform play

Your domain. Your catalog.
Your agents, smarter.

The pharma commercial catalog you just browsed is the reference build. The same engine codifies any firm's judgment — financial risk, claims adjudication, supply chain, legal review — into governed, versioned, agent-ready packs your internal AI programs subscribe to.

Stop hard-coding knowledge into agents

Build a governed domain layer agents subscribe to. One source of judgment, many agents, full provenance — change the knowledge without redeploying a single agent.

The secret sauce never ships

A two-tier boundary: the factory (mining, Forge, compiler, evals) stays inside your walls; clients receive only signed, sealed Tier-A packs. Codified judgment without exposure.

Knowledge as a versioned asset

your_domain@1.3 is an asset on the balance sheet — measured by agent-lift, not a slide in someone's folder. Prove value, then bill for it.

The packs are the product. Everything else is the factory.  The catalog is how your firm — and your clients — see the product.
